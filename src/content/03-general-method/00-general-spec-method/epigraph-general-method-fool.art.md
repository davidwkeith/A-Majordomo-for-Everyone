A padlock in 8-bit pixel art, approximately 40x48 pixels scaled up.
Three-quarter view, hasp closed, body rendered with the weight and
specificity of a real Master Lock. A bent paper clip is inserted into
the keyhole at the angle of someone who has done this before.
4-color palette drawn from the master palette: Charcoal for the lock
body, Warm Gray for the shackle and paperclip, Black for the keyhole
and outlines, Recycled for a single highlight catching the shackle's
curve. Colored in ballpoint pen and pencil per the inline-graphics
style.

**True pixel grid --- non-negotiable.** Actual 8-bit pixel art, not a
blocky illustration. Render on a literal uniform grid of axis-aligned
square pixels, as if captured from an NES framebuffer and enlarged
with nearest-neighbor scaling. Every pixel the same size, every pixel
a single flat color from the palette. Hard edges between colors, no
anti-aliasing, no smooth curves, no airbrush, no sub-pixel detail.
Diagonals are stair-stepped. The viewer should be able to count
pixels along an edge.

**No meta-elements --- non-negotiable.** The illustration contains
only the padlock and paperclip. No color swatches, palettes, legends,
keys, hex codes, callouts, arrows, sidebar text, or any UI explaining
the colors or technique. No caption text inside the image.

**Background: pure white, `#FFFFFF`, flat.** Not gray, not off-white,
not cream, not paper texture. The build removes white to create
transparency, so any gray will show as a halo in the ePub.

**Watch out for:**

- NO hand holding the paperclip --- object-focused, not figure-focused
- NO keyhole light glow or dramatic shading beyond flat palette fills
- NO padlock brand logo or readable text
- NO speech bubble, thought bubble, or dialogue text
